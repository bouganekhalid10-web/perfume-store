# ESSENCE image assets

Both images were created with the built-in imagegen tool and copied into this project. These are illustrative brand/product visuals, not photographs of inventory.

- `public/images/hero-perfume.png`: editorial still life, amber square ESSENCE Oud Royal bottle, dark olive cap, ivory limestone plinth, olive branch, warm sunlight and botanical shadows on beige plaster. Landscape framing, realistic glass and natural textures, no text outside the bottle.
- `public/images/perfume-collection.png`: one catalog strip with four equal panels: Oud Royal (amber/olive), Rose Élégance (pink/ivory), Ambre Solaire (gold), Nuit Boisée (dark green/black). Consistent frontal product photography, warm stone surfaces, soft sunlight, labels with ESSENCE and product names. The website displays individual panels using CSS background positions.

Prompt direction: premium product photography; cream, warm beige and olive palette; realistic glass reflections; minimal editorial composition; no borders or watermarks.

## Generation prompts

### Hero

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium perfume boutique website hero photograph, landscape 3:2. Create a very high-end realistic editorial still life photograph: one thick square clear amber glass perfume bottle with an elegant dark olive green cylindrical cap, small warm ivory label with precise black serif typography ESSENCE and smaller OUD ROYAL. Bottle on a warm ivory limestone plinth slightly left of center, with slender olive branch reaching from top right. Warm sun from upper left, strong late afternoon botanical shadows on pale beige plaster wall. Muted earthy creamy beige and olive color palette. Bottle large filling 65% of image height, editorial luxury perfume campaign, tactile film grain, stunning realistic glass reflections. No graphics, no borders, no text outside the bottle. Wide image, simple peaceful art direction.

### Catalog strip

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a single wide catalog strip for a perfume shop website, four exactly equal square panels side by side in one 4:1 image. This is one art-directed asset. Four high-end realistic product photographs with consistent frontal camera and matching scale. All four bottles centered within their own equal width square panel, fully visible with ample surrounding space. Panel 1: rectangular amber perfume bottle with dark olive cap and ivory label ESSENCE / OUD ROYAL, warm sand backdrop. Panel 2: blush pink glass rectangular perfume bottle with ivory round cap and small label ESSENCE / ROSE ÉLÉGANCE, muted pale pink backdrop. Panel 3: golden amber rounded rectangular perfume bottle with polished gold cap and ivory label ESSENCE / AMBRE SOLAIRE, light warm cream backdrop. Panel 4: deep dark green glass rectangular perfume bottle with black cylindrical cap and ivory label ESSENCE / NUIT BOISÉE, muted pale sage backdrop. Elegant minimal perfume product photography, realistic premium glass, sunlit soft shadows to right, subtle warm stone surfaces. No extra props, no text outside labels, no borders. Four panels exactly equal width, aspect ratio four to one.
